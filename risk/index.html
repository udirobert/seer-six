Risk

A prediction market is a claim about the future priced by people who will not have to defend it. Risk is the reading that asks what could still break: whether anyone verified the question, whether a pool exists to exit through, how far the settlement date has already slipped, and whether the answer depends on another market resolving first.

The only fourteen markets with a history

A price series is the one risk evidence you cannot argue with, and in this snapshot the API has one for fourteen markets out of —. Each line is indexed to 100 at its own first print: the pool quotes the outcome against savings xDAI, so the level would need an FX assumption while the shape and the range are the API's own. The dashed rule is 100 — the first print — so a line ending above its own beginning is a market that got more expensive, in its own units. Nothing here is smoothed, and no line is drawn where there is no pool.
